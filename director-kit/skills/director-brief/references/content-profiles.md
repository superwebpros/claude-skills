# Content Profiles — one engine, swappable framing

> The craft spine is constant for everything: uninflected shots, montage/Kuleshov, the asset layer, 4-dimension continuity, the director's-eye checklist. The **profile** only swaps the *framing* at the brief stage (goal, structure, CTA, what the dramatic object is). Pick one in `director-brief`.

| Profile | Goal | Structure | CTA | Length | Dramatic object |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **story-adaptation** | Entertain / build audience | Preserve the **source's own arc** (incl. twist/withhold) | Soft (subscribe / "read the full story") | 60s–8min | Pulled from the source (the MacGuffin, the secret) |
| **ad** | Sell | Hook → tension → **turn/promise** → CTA; anti-sag beat sheet | Hard (→ the offer, e.g. GHL) | 15–30s (+ a tight 15s) | The prospect's pain/desire vs. the objection |
| **short / reel** | Organic reach | Hook + open-loop retention; native, looser | Soft (follow / "link in bio") | 15–45s | A contrarian claim or relatable tension |
| **trailer** | Drive to buy/read | Mood + **withhold**; atmosphere over info | "Read it / get it" + title card | 30–60s | The story's central question, teased not answered |

## How a profile changes the brief
- **Logline source:** story = the source's logline; ad/short = write one (Formula B often, see story-frameworks.md); trailer = the central question.
- **The "turn":** ad = the promise/reframe lands mid-piece; story = the source's own twist; trailer = withheld.
- **CTA field:** hard vs soft vs title-card (above).
- **Project context:** load `project-context.md` for ad/short (FictionOS audience, the mid-video-sag constraint). Story/trailer for Allison's fiction lean on the source + `story-content` framing (top-of-funnel that feeds the course).
- **Format recipe:** load the matching section of `format-recipes.md`.

## What never changes (regardless of profile)
- Uninflected, sized, camera-moved shots (`director-lexicon.md`).
- Meaning built in the cut (`montage-kuleshov.md`).
- Lock the cast/props/locations/world as assets; track 4-dimension continuity (`continuity.md`).
- Run the director's-eye + continuity critic before generating; review **scene-by-scene** before spending on video.
- Generate via fal, stills-first then animate approved shots (`generation.md`).
