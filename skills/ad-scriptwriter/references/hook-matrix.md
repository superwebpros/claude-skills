## Hook Family #1: Start From Zero

* **Hook (0:00–0:04):** "If I had to build AI capability into a growing business from zero, here is the very first move I'd make."

* **Shift / Mechanism (0:13–0:26):** "Instead, I'd find *one* curious, capable person already on your payroll. The operations manager or customer lead who already knows your business intimately, and knows exactly where you bleed time."
