# Ad Voice — The Plain-Spoken Operator & Mentor

This reference defines the voice, tone, and guardrails for AI Process Pros (APP) direct-response video ads.

---

## 1. The Persona & Tone

The speaker is an experienced, battle-tested operator and educator (the APP founder, on camera) talking directly to a peer: an SMB owner, founder, or senior operator ($1M–$50M, 10–200 employees).

* **Tone:** Calm, plain-spoken, mentor-like, candid.
* **Posture:** A practitioner who has built and shipped 400+ production automations and taught non-coders at Michigan State University. Never a marketer selling an AI course or an agency pitching a retainer.
* **Relationship:** Peer-to-peer with warmth. Honest about what AI can and cannot do.

---

## 2. Word Choice & Vocabulary

### Words We Use (Operator Language)
* *Internal champion*, *curious employee*, *the person already on your payroll*
* *Duct-taped systems*, *handoffs between departments*, *messy spreadsheets*, *manual copy-pasting*
* *Operational inventory*, *connected workflows*, *trigger → context → action*
* *Own your capability*, *a pro in your corner*, *we teach the stack we ship*
* *Framework*, *roadmap*, *prioritize first*

### Words We Ban (Hype & Jargon)
* ⛔ *10x*, *supercharge*, *game-changer*, *revolutionary*, *leverage* (as a verb)
* ⛔ *Synergy*, *paradigm*, *digital transformation*, *disruption*
* ⛔ *Prompt engineering*, *prompt hacks*, *secret sauce*, *magic*
* ⛔ Developer jargon in ads: *LangGraph, MCP, vector databases, Cloudflare Workers, API endpoints, pull requests* (talk about "connecting software to computers" or "talking to your systems" instead).

---

## 3. The Anti-IT Trap (Crucial for SMB Owners)

Leading with tech stack complexity immediately loses SMB owners and their internal champions:
* **Meet the tech stack where it actually lives:** Real businesses run on Airtable, HubSpot, Google Sheets, QuickBooks, Slack, and email. Speak to making messy existing tools work together.
* **Focus on friction over code:** Frame AI around solving tedious friction (qualifying incoming leads, drafting weekly client status updates, chasing missing invoices).
* **The Champion Qualifier:** Always emphasize that the ideal champion is **not a coder**. They are an operations lead, office manager, or customer success manager who knows the business intimately.

---

## 4. Pacing & Delivery Rules

* **Spoken WPM:** 140–155 words per minute.
* **Line Breaks:** Every line break in the script represents a natural breath and camera beat.
* **Contrasts & Punchlines:** Use punchy short sentences after longer explanations:
  * *"Software doesn't create habits."*
  * *"AI can't automate a process nobody has defined."*
  * *"You own the AI. Not a vendor."*
* **Visual Punch-in:** Shift camera framing from Medium Shot (MS) to Medium Close Up (MCU) when delivering the core operational shift or mechanism.

---

## 5. Proof Levers & Regulatory Boundaries

* **MSU Proof Anchor (Tier 1 Verified):**
  > "I teach students in the College of Social Sciences at Michigan State University to build and deploy production AI workflows for real companies in a single semester. They aren't computer science majors or engineers."
* **In-House Production Automations (Tier 1 Verified):**
  > "We run over 400 production automations in our own business. We map the handoffs on paper before writing a single prompt."
* **Free vs. Paid Boundary (LOCKED):**
  * The free assessment at `/roadmap` gives **stages and gates at the method level** (the IMPACT framework) + a champion learning plan.
  * **Never promise a ranked list of prioritized opportunities in the ad**—that is the paid AI Readiness Assessment product.
* **No Guarantee on Ads:** The 3× ROI risk-reversal belongs strictly to the paid audit and flagship contract. It must never appear in top-of-funnel ad scripts.
