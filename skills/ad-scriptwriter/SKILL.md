# Ad Scriptwriter

Use this skill to draft, evaluate, and refine direct-response video ad scripts and accompanying ad copy for **AI Process Pros (APP)**. 

Unlike organic educational reels (which use soft CTAs) or long-form business essays, **ad scripts are engineered to drive qualified business owners and senior operators into a diagnostic lead magnet** (e.g. the free 2-minute Impact Assessment quiz at `/roadmap`).

---

## 1. Core Architecture: The 5-Beat Engine

Every video ad script is calibrated for **9:16 vertical video** with a target duration of **~40–50 seconds** (strictly **115–135 spoken words**, ~140–155 WPM). It follows five mandatory beats:

| Beat | Window | Word Budget | Purpose & Delivery |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Hook** | 0:00–0:04 | ~10–15 words | Scroll-stopping pattern interrupt or contrarian premise from the approved hook bank. Direct-to-camera (MS). No intro greetings. |
| **2. Agitate / Reframe** | 0:04–0:13 | ~25–35 words | Subvert conventional traps. Dismantle the false consensus (e.g., don't hire expensive AI engineers, don't buy black boxes, don't buy seats without a process). |
| **3. Shift / Mechanism** | 0:13–0:26 | ~35–45 words | The pragmatic alternative. Break the operational fix into a rule-of-three or 2-part framework. (Camera punches in to MCU). |
| **4. Proof Anchor** | 0:26–0:38 | ~25–35 words | Verified, tier-1 proof claim. (MSU SSC 490 case study or APP's 400+ production automations). |
| **5. Diagnostic CTA** | 0:38–0:48+ | ~20–25 words | Low-friction bridge to the free 2-minute diagnostic tool. Fast cut to quiz UI preview. Clear click instruction. |

## 2. The 3x3 Hook Matrix

Select one hook family and angle per script. Detailed templates are in [references/hook-matrix.md](references/hook-matrix.md).

1. **Family #1: Start From Zero**
   * *Core Premise:* "If I had to build AI capability into my business again starting from zero, here is the first move I'd make..."
   * *Sub-angles:* The Internal Champion (People) · The Bottleneck Map (Process) · Capability Ownership (Sovereignty).
2. **Family #2: Wasting Your Time**
   * *Core Premise:* "You're wasting your time if you're [common mistake] before doing [foundational step]..."
   * *Sub-angles:* Buying seats & licenses with no system · Prompt hacks vs. connected workflows · The $30k agency retainer trap.
3. **Family #3: Why It's Not Working**
   * *Core Premise:* "The reason your company's AI rollout isn't working is almost definitely this..."
   * *Sub-angles:* Lumpy adoption across departments · Chatbots with no business context/APIs · The stuck internal tinkerer learning alone on YouTube.

## 3. Brand Voice & Non-Negotiable Guardrails

Read [references/ad-voice.md](references/ad-voice.md) before drafting. These boundaries are hard rules:

* **Plain-Spoken Operator / Mentor:** Voice is calm, straight, and authoritative. Talk like a pragmatic peer who has built hundreds of systems. Zero breathless hype, no "10x", no "revolution", no "supercharge".
* **The Anti-IT Trap:** Avoid developer buzzwords (no MCP, Workers, LangGraph, vectors, API endpoints jargon). Talk about what business owners feel: *duct-taped software, spreadsheets, handoffs between departments, copy-pasting, and curious staff*.
* **The Hard ICP Gate:** The customer must have at least one curious, non-coder employee ready to learn (the "accidental champion"). If they don't, they are not a fit.
* **Proof Boundary:**
  * ✅ Citable: MSU SSC 490 (non-technical undergrads shipping production AI workflows for real companies in 14 weeks).
  * ✅ Citable: 400+ production automations running in APP's own business ("we teach the stack we ship").
  * ⛔ Refuted / Banned: Never claim "5x ROI" or global "3x ROI guarantee" on top-of-funnel ads (the 3x guarantee scopes strictly to the paid audit and flagship).
* **The Free-Artifact Boundary (LOCKED):** The free quiz at `/roadmap` delivers **stages and gates at method level** (the IMPACT framework). The *ranked possibilities and prioritized opportunities are the paid audit product*. Never promise a ranked list of opportunities in free ad copy.

## 4. Script Authoring Workflow

When tasked with generating ad scripts:

1. **Select Angle & Hook Template:** Identify the target problem and select from the 3×3 matrix.
2. **Draft the 5 Beats:** Strictly enforce the timing and word budget (115–135 words). Write out spoken dialogue cleanly.
3. **Add Stage & Visual Directions:** Include camera framing (`[VISUAL: Medium Shot (MS)]`, `[VISUAL: Punch in to MCU]`), on-screen text overlays, and B-roll/UI callouts.
4. **Draft the Accompanying Ad Copy:**
   * **Headline:** Under 6 words, punchy and curiosity-inducing.
   * **Primary Text:** 4 short, scannable paragraphs mirroring the video's premise.
   * **Description:** Crisp (e.g. "Free 2-minute assessment").
5. **Read Aloud & Verify:**
   * Does it sound like natural spoken speech?
   * Is it strictly under 135 words?
   * Does it avoid all developer jargon?
   * Is the CTA pointing to the diagnostic assessment?

Template and formatting specs are in [references/script-template.md](references/script-template.md).
